# Mechanical Engineering Portfolio ## Engineering Decision Portfolio John (Liam) DiProfio Mechanical Engineering Student | UNC Charlotte MEGR 2156 --- ## What This Portfolio Contains This portfolio documents my development as a mechanical engineer throughout MEGR 2156. Each assignment records not only the final engineering result, but also the analysis, decisions, assumptions, calculations, and evidence used to reach that result. The portfolio is organized chronologically from A1 through A12 so that a reader can follow the progression from fundamental mechanical analysis through increasingly complex design and engineering decisions. The standard for this portfolio is simple: > **Engineering is the art of making decisions you can defend.** Every assignment is evaluated through three engineering behaviors: **Analyze** — Identify the governing model, state its assumptions, and apply it to the appropriate problem. **Decide** — Compare alternatives, establish criteria, and justify the selected solution. **Communicate** — Present the reasoning and evidence clearly enough that another engineer could reproduce the work without needing additional explanation. This portfolio is intended to function as an engineering record rather than simply a collection of completed assignments. The goal is to demonstrate how engineering decisions were made, not merely what answers were obtained. --- ## About Me My name is Liam DiProfio, and Mechanical Engineering has always been a passion project for me.
